# Astonish Homepage — Build Direction

## Cobalt Aperture — Liquid Glass over a Living Void

### Big Idea
The entire homepage is ONE continuous black void with a single living cobalt intelligence at its core — a persistent R3F edge-frame cube (the Cubixso logo, never off-screen) that disassembles, migrates, blooms into a globe, and reforms across all 12 sections under the control of one scrollbar. Everything in front of the void — nav, cards, panels, the section counter, CTAs — is REFRACTIVE LIQUID GLASS that earns its brightness by bending and tinting the cobalt light and editorial type behind it, never by fill color. Three disciplines govern, in strict priority order: (1) APPLE RESTRAINT sets the rules — exactly one accent (cobalt #1B4FFF, glow #4A7BFF), near-invisible chrome, very low density, one elevation language, scale(0.95) press; (2) iOS-26 LIQUID GLASS sets the material — translucent, refractive, specular, depth-layered; (3) SAMANA kinetic scroll + ANTIGRAVITY spatial depth set the feel — weightless parallax, masked editorial reveals, momentum handoffs — woven in ONLY where they elevate, never as a sampler. The narrative thesis, "generative AI for the autonomous enterprise," is literalized: one cobalt intelligence at the center of the void, read through a family of glass instruments. The edge-frame cube is the atomic motif — the 1px cobalt hairline that draws the logo also draws every tile border, every glass corner-mark, the loader, and the closing CTA. The page opens by opening the cube's aperture and closes by closing it: one continuous breath.

### Scroll Choreography
ONE persistent fixed canvas behind all 12 sections (position:fixed; inset:0; z-0; frameloop="demand"): the void + the cobalt cube/particle field, driven by a SINGLE normalized scroll progress p∈[0,1] read off the existing Lenis instance (window.scrollY already synced) → one GSAP ScrollTrigger master timeline for the cube + Framer useScroll/useInView per section. The cube is the spine and does ALL heavy 3D so DOM sections stay cheap glass: it migrates and morphs by section (Hero center-ignite → drifts as particles disassemble for Manifesto → recedes deep for Services → tightens to a node-cluster for KineticStack → blooms into a wireframe globe for Global → reforms to the clean edge-frame logo at FinalCTA), emissive intensity breathing per section. Cube transforms are DAMPED (lerp toward target each frame) so the object always trails scroll slightly — that lag is the mass-in-zero-g cue. DEPTH LAW (the antigravity signature, free parallax): every DOM element declares a depth plane 0–3 and is translated y = p·-depthSpeed via translate3d only — back planes (particles, glass veils) move 0.2–0.4×, foreground type/chips move 1.0–1.1×. Sections are glass slabs that arrive via a signature 3-BEAT REVEAL: (1) MASK-WIPE — content opens from behind a clip-path inset on --ease-out-expo as the section crosses 35% viewport; (2) LIFT — slab translateY(40px→0) while a 1px specular highlight sweeps its top edge once; (3) SETTLE — headline does the existing animate-text kinetic word-build, staggered 40–60ms/word, overshooting once then relaxing. HANDOFF: leaving slabs scale to 0.96 + drop to 70% brightness as the incoming slab clips up over them (no hard cuts), and the outgoing tile's cobalt edge-frame BLINKS once at the seam — the recurring "aperture blink." PINNED BEATS, capped at THREE to protect rhythm and perf (Apple cadence over conveyor): KineticStack (cube collapses to a node-graph, AI inference pulses), Work (masked tile-handoff product gallery), and Global (cube blooms to globe). The void is NEVER covered edge-to-edge — a 12–24px black gutter rings every slab so the light always lives behind it. A 1px cobalt scroll-rail on the right edge fills as a continuous aperture line + doubles as the gravity gauge; a tiny fixed glass counter pill (01/12…12/12) lower-left refracts whatever scrolls under it. Lenis lerp ≈0.09 for the buttery weightless glide; horizontal scroll is never hijacked. REDUCED-MOTION: cube freezes to one elegant static pose, the canvas drops to a single still frame, mask-wipes/handoffs/skew/parallax/pinning all collapse to 200ms opacity fades with IDENTICAL final layout — but the (CSS, cheap) glass material stays.

### Liquid-Glass System
ONE refractive Liquid Glass family REPLACES all shadows as the sole elevation language, built from a single edge-frame primitive so nav, cards, and panels read as one system. Base token: --glass = background color-mix(in oklab, var(--color-bg-soft) 58%, transparent); backdrop-filter: blur(20px) saturate(160%) brightness(1.06); a 1px inner TOP specular rim rgba(255,255,255,.14) + 1px inner BOTTOM lens-edge rgba(0,0,0,.4); the cobalt edge-frame corner-mark (2 L-shaped 1px cobalt strokes in opposite corners). Four tiers: TIER 1 NAV/RAIL (chrome) — the floating glass pill; on scroll-down it compresses height + raises blur to 28px so content visibly bends/brightens behind it; one 120° specular sweep crosses it on load and on re-pin. TIER 2 PANEL (Services, Work, Industries, BackedBy cards) — blur(28px) saturate(160%); top→bottom white .06→.02 light-fall-off gradient; inset highlight ring via box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,.16), inset 0 0 0 1px var(--color-line); pointer-tracked cobalt rim-light (--mx/--my spotlight, inset 0 0 0 1px color-mix(accent-2 18%)) that intensifies toward the cursor; radius 18px; cobalt corner-mark lights up when active. TIER 3 LENS (KineticStack inference panel + Testimonial ONLY — the 2 "wow" surfaces) — a true refractive lens: SVG feDisplacementMap (turbulence) subtly distorting/chromatically shifting what's behind, plus the specular sweep. This is the ONLY tier that distorts geometry and the costliest piece, so it is hard-capped at 2 instances and IntersectionObserver-paused off-screen. TIER 4 CHIP (mono tags, status pills, the section counter, CTAs) — blur(12px) saturate(150%); pill radius; cobalt text/stroke. CTAs = cobalt-filled liquid-glass pill with a specular top sheen + press-into-liquid (+4px blur for one frame on :active). REFRACTION RULES: glass is NEVER given an opaque fill or a color of its own — its visible tint is 100% borrowed from the cobalt cube + type behind it (so a card looks faintly blue near the cube, neutral far from it — automatic on-brand cohesion); cobalt enters ONLY via the rim-light and the accent. EXACTLY ONE real shadow exists in the whole system (Apple law): 0 1px 40px rgba(0,0,40,.5) under the single elevated focal Tier-2 panel per section — never on type, buttons, tiles, or chrome; all other depth = blur + specular rim + void gutter + surface-lightness step. Every glass element carries the two signatures (1px top specular rim + cobalt edge-frame corner-mark) so 12 sections are one family. FALLBACK: no-backdrop-filter / low-DPR degrades to flat rgba(20,22,30,.7) (or color-mix(--color-bg-soft 92%)) + the 1px hairline — still on-brand, zero jank.

### Motion Language
THREE eases only (already tokenized): --ease-out-expo (0.16,1,0.3,1) for reveals/glass lifts/camera (authoritative arrival), --ease-spring (0.34,1.56,0.64,1) ONLY for press/pop micro-interactions and the cube/logo snap (the brand's beating heart), --ease-smooth (0.22,1,0.36,1) for cube damping, parallax, and cross-fades. FOUR motion verbs, nothing outside this vocabulary (signature, not sampler): (a) MASK — clip-path inset wipes keyed to the cube edge-frame direction (headlines wipe up, columns from the leading edge); the universal entrance, never a plain fade except in reduced-motion. (b) DRIFT/FLOAT — antigravity parallax on depth planes plus a tiny perpetual idle float (±6px, 7–9s ease-in-out) so the page feels buoyant at rest; pure translate3d. (c) SWEEP — a single 1px-wide white→transparent gradient traveling once across a glass surface on entrance and on hover ("light catching the lens"), driven by an animated --sweep custom property on ::before, transform-only. (d) SETTLE/PRESS — arrivals overshoot once then relax; every interactive element scales to 0.95 on :active with --ease-spring and its specular rim brightens (Apple's exact micro-interaction), +4px blur bump on glass. KINETIC WORD-BUILD (the existing animate-text) is reserved for exactly THREE moments (Hero word-build, Manifesto line, FinalCTA) so it stays a signature, not a tic. A single Lenis-derived --vel var feeds ALL velocity-reactive skew (≤1.2°) and blur-stretch on fast flicks, capped and relaxed within 300ms of scroll stop — one momentum personality. The magnetic cursor (already built) gently tugs CTAs and the cube toward the pointer for the antigravity attraction feel; disabled on coarse/touch pointers. Cadence is slow and confident: reveals 0.7s, sweeps 0.9s, handoffs 0.5s, cube damping ~0.6s settle, micro-press 0.12s — nothing snappy except the press. Strictly transform/opacity/filter (GPU); GSAP ScrollTrigger drives the 3 pins + cube progress, Framer drives in-view slab reveals, R3F drives only the cube. Hover is desktop-only (fine pointer), limited to sweep + rim brighten + rim-light spotlight — no layout shift, ever. REDUCED-MOTION: all of the above collapse to a 0.2s opacity fade; cube holds a pose, canvas freezes to one frame; sweeps/parallax/skew/pinning disabled.

### Color & Contrast
Dark-dominant void with a single cobalt light and rare earned light tiles. SURFACE: ~10 of 12 sections ride --color-bg #000 / --color-bg-soft #0a0a0a / --color-surface #161617, with a micro-step lightness change between adjacent dark slabs (Apple's tile-1/2/3 trick) so seams read without heavy borders. EXACTLY ONE-to-TWO earned light moments — Metrics (proof) and optionally the FinalCTA inner card — flip to the existing html[data-theme=light] tokens (--color-bg #f5f5f7, ink --color-fg #0a0a0c) via a real token swap, not a hack; the surface inversion itself IS the divider and lands like a spotlight snapping on after seven near-black tiles. ACCENT LAW (Apple one-color, dark-brand variant): --color-accent #1B4FFF is the ONLY interactive/brand light — every rim-light, scroll-rail, reveal frame, active state, pulse, and primary CTA. --color-accent-2 #4A7BFF is emissive/glow ONLY (particle glow, rim-light bloom, specular tint, and the legibility step for small cobalt text on dark — the Apple "sky-link on dark" move) — never a second brand color. --color-accent-3 #0028A8 is shadow-tone depth inside the cube and glass undersides. --color-signal #ff3950 is ALARM-ONLY and appears AT MOST ONCE on the entire page (the Metrics "LIVE" status dot) — nowhere else. CONTRAST: body on dark uses --color-fg #fff and --color-fg-2 #a1a1a6 (≥4.5:1); on the light tile, ink #0a0a0c. Glass NEVER lowers text contrast — type sits ON glass at full token contrast; glass only blurs what's BEHIND it, and the slab's effective backdrop is kept dark so type-over-glass verifies ≥4.5:1. Cube glass tint stays cool-neutral (#c2d8ff), never warm. Grain (0.06 overlay) + hairline-grid stay as the atmospheric tissue tying every slab to the cube-edge aesthetic.

### Global Rules
1) ONE ACCENT: cobalt #1B4FFF (glow #4A7BFF, deep #0028A8) is the only interactive/brand light; accent-2 is emissive/legibility-step ONLY, #ff3950 signal is alarm-only and appears AT MOST ONCE (Metrics LIVE dot) — nothing else is ever clickable-colored. 2) ONE ELEVATION LANGUAGE: refractive Liquid Glass (4-tier scale) replaces all shadows; no drop-shadows on chrome, cards, buttons, type, or tiles — depth comes from blur + specular rim + void gutter + surface-lightness step. EXACTLY ONE real shadow exists (0 1px 40px rgba(0,0,40,.5)) under the single focal Tier-2 panel per section, never elsewhere. 3) ONE VOID: a single fixed R3F canvas behind all 12 sections; sections never paint edge-to-edge opaque — always a 12–24px black gutter so the cobalt light lives behind the glass; the edge-frame cube is the ONE persistent hero object, visible in some form in every section — never a second competing 3D motif. 4) EDGE-FRAME PRIMITIVE: the 1px cobalt/line hairline that draws the cube also draws every slab, glass panel, reveal mask, and corner-mark; only chrome/panel glass and CTAs get radius (18px cards, pill CTAs). 5) FOUR MOTION VERBS ONLY (MASK, DRIFT/FLOAT, SWEEP, SETTLE/PRESS) + THREE eases (--ease-out-expo reveals, --ease-spring press/cube, --ease-smooth damping/parallax) — no effect outside this vocabulary. Kinetic word-build limited to 3 moments (Hero, Manifesto, FinalCTA); pinned sections capped at 3 (KineticStack, Work, Global); Tier-3 displacement lenses capped at 2. 6) PERFORMANCE: all motion transform/opacity/filter only (no width/height/top/left); will-change added on ScrollTrigger enter and stripped on leave; backdrop-filter capped (≤40% viewport, slab-level not row-level, ≤3 simultaneous blur panels on screen — rest fall back to flat --color-surface); ONE fixed R3F canvas with frameloop="demand"+invalidate() on scroll/pointer, dpr={[1,2]}, Draco-compressed cube, particles ≤2–3k and culled/throttled to 30fps off-screen, paused on hidden tab, single env/BakeShadows; Tier-3 lenses IntersectionObserver-paused off-screen; one Lenis --vel var caps skew ≤1.2° and relaxes within 300ms; target ≥60fps desktop, Lighthouse ≥80. 7) REDUCED-MOTION SAFE BY CONSTRUCTION: prefers-reduced-motion freezes the cube to a pose, drops the canvas to one still frame, disables pin/clip/skew/parallax/sweep, and swaps to 200ms opacity fades with IDENTICAL final layout; no-backdrop-filter fallback flattens glass to color-mix + 1px line. 8) ACCESSIBILITY: real semantic sr-only <h1> per section, decorative spread type aria-hidden; focus-visible uses the existing 2px --color-accent-2 ring; keyboard nav never blocked by Lenis; magnetic cursor off on coarse/touch; type-over-glass verified ≥4.5:1 by keeping the effective backdrop dark; small cobalt text steps to accent-2 on dark. 9) DENSITY STAYS VERY LOW (Apple): one idea per slab, ≥64px of air above headlines, two CTAs max per view, the footer the only permitted dense moment. 10) TOKEN-ONLY: use --color-*, --text-* ramp, --pad-x, --col-w, --ease-* — never inline hex or ad-hoc easing or invented sizes; SF Pro Display headlines weight 600 with negative tracking ('Apple tight'), body 17px/1.47, mono 11px/0.18em uppercase labels/counter, no weight 500. The result is a signature, not a grab-bag: one void, one cobalt intelligence, read through one family of refractive glass instruments — opened and closed by the cube's aperture.
