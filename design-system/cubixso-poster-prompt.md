# Nano Banana Pro — Cubixso Education-Tech Poster Prompt

**How to use:** Upload your Cubixso cube logo as a reference image, paste the prompt
below, set aspect ratio to **2:3 (vertical poster)**. If any word renders slightly
wrong, re-run or add: "fix spelling of <word>, render all text crisp and correct."

---

## PROMPT

Create a premium vertical "Thank You" appreciation poster (2:3, 1080×1620) for
an **AI-native global technology company**, using the uploaded Cubixso cube
logo. The mood is sophisticated enterprise-AI, like an Apple keynote meets a
deep-tech brand — absolutely NOT childish or cartoon school style.

VISUAL STYLE — Luxury Palette 05 (Cosmos Blue / Crimson / Cream):
- Background: rich deep Cosmos Blue (#002F49) gradient deepening to near-black,
  with a faint starfield/particle texture and soft glow pools.
- Surfaces: liquid-glass / glassmorphism panels — translucent, frosted, with thin
  luminous 1px borders and subtle inner highlights, tinted toward warm cream.
- Colors: text mostly warm Cream / Varden (#FEF0D5); primary accent Crimson
  Blaze (#C1121F) for emphasis words, key titles and the rocket; headings in a
  cream-to-crimson gradient. Keep it restrained — cream is the body, crimson is
  the spark, cosmos blue is the depth.
- Typography: modern geometric sans-serif (SF Pro / Inter feel), high contrast,
  generous spacing, perfectly legible. Elegant italic serif for sub-lines.
- Icons: sleek glossy 3D glass-and-metal tech icons inside rounded glass tiles
  (cream + crimson tones) — NOT flat colorful emoji, NOT clip-art.

LAYOUT, top to bottom:

1. TOP: the uploaded Cubixso cube logo, centered, glowing softly, with the
   wordmark "Cubixso" beside it. Directly under, a small uppercase
   letter-spaced mono line: "HYDERABAD · USA · UAE".

2. HERO: an oversized cream-to-crimson gradient "Thank You" as the focal
   headline. Beneath it, in elegant italic serif cream: "for building the
   AI-native future with us." Then a crisp cream line: "Cubixso Solutions — an
   AI-Native Technology Company", and under it smaller: "Education Tech · Global
   Products · Bright Minds. Bright Features."

3. A glass pill banner bar reading: "End-to-End Solutions for Indian Educational
   Institutes".

4. PRODUCT STACK — five horizontal glass cards, each with a distinct 3D glossy
   tech icon in a rounded glass tile on the left, a crimson title, and a cream
   subtitle:
   - (smart display / digital board icon) "Digital Boards & Smart Classroom
     Solutions"
   - (modern school building icon) "NXT Schools — AI-Automated School Management
     Software"
   - (graduation cap icon) "NXT Online Tuitions — for Students of All Classes"
   - (friendly AI robot head icon) "NXT AI Nestam — Learn AI the Fun & Easy Way"
   - (AI teacher / headset avatar icon) "NXT — 24/7 AI Teacher for All Classes"

5. FOOTER: a thin "by" divider, then bold wordmark "CUBIXSO Solutions Pvt. Ltd.",
   and below it in italic serif: "An AI-Native Global Technology Company".

6. BOTTOM BANNER: a deep cosmos-blue glass strip with a glowing crimson rocket,
   reading: "Transforming Education Through Innovation, Automation & Artificial
   Intelligence." with "Innovation, Automation & Artificial Intelligence"
   emphasized in crimson.

Render every line sharply legible and correctly spelled, balanced and luxurious,
with cinematic depth and a premium global-tech identity.
