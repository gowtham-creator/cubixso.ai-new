## 5. Harminder Singh — Senior Advocate, Punjab High Court

> Research that used to mean days in the archive now takes an afternoon. Law
> Suit GPT searches across forty thousand judgments and returns the passage, not
> just the case. For a practising advocate, a citation arriving in seconds
> instead of hours changes how you prepare.

**Two things to check before this one goes anywhere:**

1. **The court's name.** India's court at Chandigarh is the *Punjab & Haryana
   High Court*; there is no separate "Punjab High Court". Confirm his correct
   designation with him — a wrong court title on a practising advocate's public
   testimonial is the kind of error that gets noticed.
2. **"Senior Advocate" is a formal designation** conferred by a High Court or
   the Supreme Court under the Advocates Act — it is not a seniority adjective.
   If he has not been so designated, the title should read "Advocate".

## Before any of these are published

Each quote is attributed to a real, named person. Cubixso drafting a testimonial
for a client to approve is ordinary practice; publishing one the person has not
seen is not — it is a fabricated endorsement, and for the advocate in item 5 it
carries professional risk for him as well as reputational risk for Cubixso.

Send each person their own quote, let them edit it, and publish exactly what
comes back. Keep the approval (an email or message) on file.
