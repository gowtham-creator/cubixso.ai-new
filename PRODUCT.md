# PRODUCT.md

## Register

**brand** — cubixso.ai is a marketing site; the design is the first product a lead evaluates.

## Product purpose

Cubixso.ai (Cubixso Solutions Private Limited) is an AI-native engineering studio, HQ at T-Hub Hyderabad, with presence in the UAE and a US office opening. It designs, builds and hands over production AI systems: agents and their harnesses, retrieval and knowledge systems, LLMOps, full AI products, and AI storytelling pipelines.

The site's job is to turn evaluation into a scoping call. A visitor should leave knowing exactly what Cubixso builds, how it builds it, and why it can be trusted with production.

## Users

1. **Enterprise technical buyers** — CTOs, VPs of Engineering, heads of data. Weighing a build partner. They want architecture, method, failure modes, proof. Next step: book a scoping call.
2. **Founders and product leaders** — startups and scale-ups that want an AI product built. They want outcomes, examples, speed. Next step: start a project.
3. **Creative and media teams** — exploring AI storytelling and video generation. They want to know what is real today. Next step: start a project.

## Brand personality

Three words: **precise, candid, engineered.**

The README's voice rule stands: plain, specific, unhyped. Short sentences. Name the thing.

> Honest scoping, real timelines. We hand you a system, not a notebook.

## Anti-references

- "AI revolution" landing pages: gradient orbs, glowing brains, robot stock art.
- Buzzword grids of identical icon cards ("Scalable · Secure · Seamless").
- Invented metrics, fake logos, testimonials nobody approved.
- Vague capability lists with no method, no failure modes, no proof.

## Design principles

The six from the README's design language, plus one:

1. **One accent.** Cobalt earns attention. If everything is cobalt, nothing is.
2. **Glass, not shadow.** Elevation is backdrop blur and rim light, used sparingly.
3. **Gradients live in the void and inside type**, never as a fill on a glass surface.
4. **Motion is scroll-linked, not decorative.**
5. **Contrast floors are non-negotiable.** Every text token clears WCAG AA.
6. **Degrade on purpose.** Weak GPU, reduced motion, no WebGL, blocked autoplay: each has a fallback.
7. **Claim only what's shipped.** Capabilities are described as they are delivered today. In-build work is labelled as such. Numbers appear only when they are real and published.

## Accessibility

WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, full keyboard operation with visible focus, `prefers-reduced-motion` honoured by every animation system, semantic landmarks and headings.
